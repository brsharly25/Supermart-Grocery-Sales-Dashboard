# Supermart Grocery Sales Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive retail analytics dashboard built using **Microsoft Power BI** to examine sales, profitability, product performance, discount patterns, and geographic results. The project translates transaction-level sales data into practical business insights and recommendations.

## Project objective

Identify sales and profit trends, evaluate the performance of product categories and sub-categories, compare regions and cities, and support data-driven retail decisions.

## Tools and skills

- **Power BI Desktop** — report development and interactive data visualization
- **DAX** — business measures and KPI calculations
- **Retail / business analytics** — sales, profit, orders, discount, and geographic analysis
- **Data storytelling** — findings and recommendations for decision-makers

## Dashboard pages

1. **Executive Overview** — overall sales, total profit, orders, profit margin, average discount, and sales breakdowns.
2. **Product Performance** — category and sub-category comparisons, profitability, and discount analysis.
3. **Geographic Performance** — region, state, and city-level sales and profit comparisons.
4. **Insights and Recommendations** — key observations and proposed business actions.

## Selected business insights

- Annual sales rose from **2.98M in 2015** to **4.98M in 2018**.
- **Eggs, Meat & Fish** led category sales at **2.27M**.
- **Snacks** led category profit at **568,179**, while **Health Drinks** led sub-category sales at **1.05M**.
- The **West region** delivered the highest regional sales (**4.80M**) and profit (**1.19M**).
- **Kanyakumari** led city sales at **706,764**, but its **24.37%** profit margin was below the overall margin of **25.05%**.

## Business recommendations

1. Maintain availability in strong-selling product lines and investigate what drives profitability in Snacks.
2. Compare discounts and profit margins by sub-category before adjusting promotions; the visual comparison alone does not demonstrate causation.
3. Examine why Kanyakumari's margin is below average, and compare with higher-margin cities.
4. Track sales, margin, product contribution, and regional performance regularly.
